Executive Order 14421 prohibits foreign-made equipment that poses a national security risk to the U.S. bulk-power system — and pushes provenance requirements down to 69 kV, below the NERC BES threshold. This brief breaks down what the order covers, why CIP-013 and CIP-010 attestations don't close the gap, and how firmware analysis builds the provenance record DOE's December rules will ask for.
